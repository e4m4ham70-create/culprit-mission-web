피해자 증언 : 홍길순(3학년 6반 담임 선생님)
I was looking at our students talking to each other. Suddenly, the window shattered, and a baseball flew in. I saw the student wear a cap.
💡 shattered = 깨졌다 / flew in = 날아왔다

💡 suspect = 용의자 / on my way back to = ~로 돌아가는 길에
